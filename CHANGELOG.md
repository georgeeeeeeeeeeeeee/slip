# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-21

First release: AU and VST3 for macOS 11+ (universal: Apple silicon and Intel), VST3 for 64-bit Windows.

- Records the track all the time; a gesture moves a second playhead, pitch and time together.
- Gestures: Hold, Reverse, Jog (springs back), Scratch, and Fire for a Brake, Backspin, Half speed
  or Rewind lasting from a quarter beat to two bars.
- Snap back at once, or on the next beat or bar line, with a short crossfade so it doesn't click.
- A platter in the window: drag the record to scratch, turn the ring to jog.
- Plays from Live's Key Map and MIDI Map, MIDI notes and the pitch wheel. Mapped controls light up
  in the window as they move.
